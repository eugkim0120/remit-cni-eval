REMIT Disclosure Rules for an Unplanned Outage

The rule under test and each agent's behaviour.

1

Power Plant Outage

07:52: output falls from 400 MW to 100 MW. Only the company knows.

The desk has sold 300 MW for tomorrow and must buy 200 MW back.

2

Inside Information

Less supply means higher prices once the outage is public.

Buying before then exploits sellers who don't know.

3

REMIT Obligations

Disclose (Art 4)Publish an Urgent Market Message promptly.
No insider trading (Art 3)Don't trade on undisclosed information.

GB/EU energy market-abuse law, enforced by Ofgem in GB.

Example UMM

URGENT MARKET MESSAGE · PUBLIC
Unit

From

Cause

Seen by all market participants at once.
Availability declaration ≠ UMM. The declaration informs the grid operator; only the UMM informs the market.
